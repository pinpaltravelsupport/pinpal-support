Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Our Privacy Commitment

At Pinpal: US Travel Scrapbook ("Pinpal", "we", "us"), we believe that your travel history, personal photos, and bucket-list memories belong exclusively to you. Pinpal is architected as a 100% Local-First application, engineered so that your personal photo albums and travel records reside directly on your personal device with zero account registration, zero advertising trackers, and zero data harvesting.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Personal Photos & Travel Scrapbook Memories: When you add photos or travel memories to a state album or trip itinerary, they are compressed and stored locally on your device using client-side IndexedDB storage. Your personal photo files are never uploaded to external servers, analyzed by AI vision models, or shared with third parties.

Zero Biometric Information Collection (BIPA Compliance): Pinpal does not collect, capture, store, or process biometric identifiers or biometric information (as defined under the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act, 740 ILCS 14/, the Texas Capture or Use of Biometric Identifier Act, or similar state laws), nor does it extract facial geometry. We perform zero automated facial recognition or facial landmark detection on your travel photos.

Zero GPS Tracking: Pinpal does not request, access, or track your device's real-time GPS location. All visited states, cities, national parks, and landmarks are recorded exclusively by manual user selection. We collect zero background or real-time location telemetry.

Aviation & Road Trip Data: Flights and road trip itineraries entered into your personal logbook are stored strictly on your device to calculate miles and render schematic map paths.

100% On-Device Local Notifications: Optional upcoming trip reminders and "On This Day" memory throwbacks are scheduled exclusively on your device using Apple's local notification framework (UNUserNotificationCenter). Pinpal operates zero remote push notification servers, does not collect Apple Push Notification service (APNs) device tokens, and never transmits your trip dates or notification schedules off your device.

3. On-Device Storage & Portable JSON Backups

On iOS, Pinpal operates completely offline on your device and does not transmit your travel data to external servers. To back up or transfer your complete travel history and photo library between devices, you can export a portable JSON backup file anytime using the built-in Export Data tool and save it to iCloud Drive, Files, or AirDrop.

4. In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit)

The optional Explorer Pass Lifetime upgrade is processed directly through Apple's App Store via StoreKit 2. Pinpal never collects, handles, or stores your credit card details, bank account information, or billing address. All billing and transaction records are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention, Portability & Deletion

You have full sovereignty over your travel data at all times:

  • Deletion: You can delete individual photos, flights, trips, or state records directly in the app. Tapping Reset All Progress & Data permanently erases all local records immediately, or you may uninstall the app to remove all local app storage.
  • Data Portability: You can export your complete travel history and photo library into an unencrypted, portable JSON file at any time using the Export Data tool in the Data Backup modal.

6. Zero Third-Party Wiretapping, Trackers & Multi-State Privacy Rights

Anti-Wiretapping & Zero Pen-Register Certification (CIPA Compliance): Pinpal does not embed any third-party session-replay software, keystroke recorders, chat-bot wiretaps, spy pixels, cross-site tracking cookies, or pen-register / trap-and-trace beacons within the meaning of the California Invasion of Privacy Act (Cal. Penal Code §§ 631, 638.51), the Federal Wiretap Act, or state wiretapping statutes.

US State Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA, VCDPA, CPA, CTDPA, TDPSA & GDPR): Pinpal does not incorporate third-party advertising SDKs or data-broker libraries. We do not "sell" or "share" (for cross-context behavioral advertising) your personal information, sensitive personal information, or geolocation data.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Pinpal does not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where applicable by jurisdiction). Because our application operates on a local-first basis without user account registration, no identifiable child data is harvested or retained.
