📍 Pinpal Privacy Center

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Our Privacy Commitment

At Pinpal: US Travel Map & Memory Vault ("Pinpal", "we", "us"), we believe that your travel history, personal photos, and bucket-list memories belong exclusively to you. Pinpal is architected as a Local-First application, engineered so that your personal data resides on your personal device.

2. Information We Handle (and Do Not Collect)

Personal Photos & Travel Lore: When you add photos or travel memories to a state album, they are compressed and stored locally on your device using client-side IndexedDB storage. Your photos are not uploaded to public databases, analyzed by AI vision models, or shared with third parties.

Zero GPS Tracking: Pinpal does not request, access, or track your device's real-time GPS location. All visited states, cities, national parks, and landmarks are recorded exclusively by manual user selection. We collect zero background or real-time location telemetry.

Aviation Data: Flights entered into your personal logbook are stored in your device storage to calculate total air miles and render flight paths.

3. Optional Cloud Sync

If you choose to use the optional Cloud Sync feature to synchronize your travel map across your personal devices (e.g., iPhone, iPad, Mac), your travel summary is transmitted via encrypted HTTPS to our secure sync endpoint. Cloud Sync is identified strictly by an anonymous, user-generated Sync Key. No email address, phone number, real name, or social media profile is required to use Cloud Sync.

4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

All in-app purchases and Explorer Pass subscriptions are processed directly through Apple's App Store via StoreKit. Pinpal never collects, handles, or stores your credit card details, bank account information, or billing address. All billing and transaction records are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention, Portability & Deletion

You have full sovereignty over your travel data at all times:

  • Deletion: You can delete individual photos, flights, or state records directly in the app. Tapping the Reset Progress (🔄) button in the app header allows you to permanently erase all local records immediately.
  • Data Portability: You can export your complete travel history and photo metadata into an unencrypted, portable JSON file at any time using the Export Data Backup tool in the settings drawer.

6. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Pinpal does not incorporate third-party advertising SDKs, cross-app trackers, or data-broker libraries. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) or European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Pinpal does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. Because our application operates on a local-first basis without user account registration, no identifiable child data is harvested or retained.
