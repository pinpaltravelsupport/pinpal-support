Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Our Privacy Commitment

At Pinpal: US Travel Scrapbook ("Pinpal", "we", "us"), we believe that your travel history, personal photos, and bucket-list memories belong exclusively to you. Pinpal is architected as a Local-First application, engineered so that your personal photo albums and travel records reside on your personal device.

2. Information We Handle (and Do Not Collect)

Personal Photos & Travel Scrapbook Memories: When you add photos or travel memories to a state album, they are compressed and stored locally on your device using client-side IndexedDB storage. Your personal photo files are never uploaded to our Cloud Sync servers, analyzed by AI vision models, or shared with third parties.

Zero Biometric Information Collection (BIPA Compliance): Pinpal does not collect, capture, store, or process biometric identifiers or biometric information (as defined under the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act, 740 ILCS 14/, the Texas Capture or Use of Biometric Identifier Act, or similar state laws), nor does it extract facial geometry. We perform zero automated facial recognition, facial landmark detection, or artificial intelligence vision analysis on your uploaded travel photos. Photos remain static local image files stored solely inside your device's client storage (IndexedDB).

Zero GPS Tracking: Pinpal does not request, access, or track your device's real-time GPS location. All visited states, cities, national parks, and landmarks are recorded exclusively by manual user selection. We collect zero background or real-time location telemetry.

Aviation Data: Flights entered into your personal logbook are stored in your device storage to calculate total air miles and render schematic flight paths.

3. Optional Cloud Sync & Bearer Sync Key Security

Pinpal automatically generates a unique, random per-device Sync Key (e.g., pinpal-xxxxxxxx) so you can optionally synchronize your travel map across your personal devices (e.g., iPhone, iPad, Mac/PC) without creating an account, password, or providing an email address, phone number, or real name.

  • Metadata-Only Synchronization: Cloud Sync transmits only lightweight map and checklist metadata (visited state statuses, cities, landmark checklists, flight logbook entries, and road trip stops). Personal photo files are automatically stripped prior to Cloud Sync transmission and remain 100% on your local device (to transfer photos between devices, use the local Export Data Backup JSON file tool).
  • Bearer Key Responsibility: Your Sync Key functions as an anonymous bearer token. Anyone who knows your exact Sync Key can view or merge the travel checklist metadata associated with that key. Do not replace your auto-generated Sync Key with easily guessable common words (such as your first name or "test"), and keep your Sync Key confidential.

4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

All in-app purchases and Explorer Pass subscriptions are processed directly through Apple's App Store via StoreKit. Pinpal never collects, handles, or stores your credit card details, bank account information, or billing address. All billing and transaction records are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention, Portability & Deletion

You have full sovereignty over your travel data at all times:

  • Deletion: You can delete individual photos, flights, trips, or state records directly in the app. Tapping the Reset All Data button in the app settings allows you to permanently erase all local records immediately. You may also request deletion of any Cloud Sync profile key by emailing support.
  • Data Portability: You can export your complete travel history and photo library into an unencrypted, portable JSON file at any time using the Export Data Backup tool in the Cloud Sync & Backup modal.

6. Zero Third-Party Wiretapping, Trackers & Multi-State Privacy Rights

Anti-Wiretapping & Zero Pen-Register Certification (CIPA Compliance): Pinpal does not embed any third-party session-replay software, keystroke recorders, chat-bot wiretaps, spy pixels, cross-site tracking cookies, or pen-register / trap-and-trace beacons within the meaning of the California Invasion of Privacy Act (Cal. Penal Code §§ 631, 638.51), the Federal Wiretap Act, or state wiretapping statutes.

US State Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA, VCDPA, CPA, CTDPA, TDPSA & GDPR): Pinpal does not incorporate third-party advertising SDKs or data-broker libraries. We do not "sell" or "share" (for cross-context behavioral advertising) your personal information, sensitive personal information, or geolocation data under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA), Colorado Privacy Act (CPA), Connecticut Data Privacy Act (CTDPA), Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA), or European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Pinpal does not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where applicable by jurisdiction). Because our application operates on a local-first basis without user account registration, no identifiable child data is harvested or retained.
