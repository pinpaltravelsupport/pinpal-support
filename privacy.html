Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Our Privacy Commitment

At Pinpal: US Travel Map & Memory Vault, your privacy is fundamental. We believe your travel memories, visited locations, and personal photos belong exclusively to you. Pinpal is architected as a Local-First application, meaning your personal data stays on your personal device.

2. Information We Collect (and Do Not Collect)

Photos & Trip Notes: When you add photos or travel lore to a state, they are processed and stored locally on your device via client-side IndexedDB storage. They are not uploaded to public databases or shared with third parties.

Location Data: If you use the optional GPS check-in feature, location coordinates are requested solely to determine which U.S. state or territory you are currently located in. We do not track background GPS, log persistent route trails, or sell location data to data brokers.

Aviation Data: Flights logged in your personal logbook are stored in your device storage to calculate air miles and draw flight radar arcs.

3. Optional Cloud Sync

If you choose to use the Cloud Sync feature to sync between multiple personal devices (such as your iPhone and iPad), your encrypted travel record is transmitted via HTTPS to our secure sync endpoint, identified strictly by your private, anonymous Sync Key. No real name, phone number, or social media login is required.

4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

All in-app purchases and Explorer Pass subscriptions are processed directly through Apple's App Store via StoreKit. Pinpal never collects, handles, or stores your credit card number, billing address, or payment credentials.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You maintain complete control over your data at all times. You can delete individual photos, flights, or state records directly in the app. Tapping the "Reset All Progress" button in settings permanently deletes all local records immediately.
