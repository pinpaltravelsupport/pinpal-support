❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Explorer Pass Lifetime? Are there any monthly fees?
The Explorer Pass Lifetime ($9.99) is a one-time purchase with $0 recurring subscription fees forever. Pay once to permanently unlock unlimited state photo scrapbook vaults, Flight Radar & 177 airport hubs, 50 American Wonders, 50 State High Points, Road Trip Planner, and VIP Share Studio exports across your Apple Account.
What happens if I get a new iPhone or switch devices?
Your purchase and travel scrapbook are fully portable:
  • Restoring Your Explorer Pass: Open Pinpal, tap the 👑 Explorer Pass card or Data Backup menu, and tap Restore Purchases. Apple StoreKit will verify your Apple Account and unlock your Lifetime Pass immediately at no cost.
  • Transferring Your Travel Scrapbook: Open the Data Backup menu in the top bar and tap Export Data to save a portable JSON backup file (including your states, checklists, flights, and photos) to iCloud Drive, Files, or AirDrop, then tap Import Data on your new device.
How does the 1 Free Photo slot work?
Every traveler can track all 50 states, 700+ cities, and 63 U.S. National Parks for free, plus add 1 free state cover photo to experience the interactive Photo Map. Upgrading to the one-time Explorer Pass Lifetime unlocks unlimited photo vaults across all 50 states and all premium landmark & aviation tools.
Are my trip photos and location data kept private?
Yes, 100%. Pinpal is built on a local-first privacy architecture. Your photos, flight logs, and travel notes are stored locally on your device. Pinpal does not use background GPS tracking, requires no account or login, never uploads your personal photo files to external servers, and never sells or shares your personal data with third parties.
How do I save Share Studio cards to my Camera Roll?
Open the Share Studio from the top header, choose your card style (Map, Recap, Snapshot Vault, Boarding Pass, or Itinerary), and tap Download Image or Share to Apps, then select Save Image in the iOS share sheet to save directly to your Photos library.
How do I request a refund?
Because all financial transactions are processed directly by Apple's App Store via StoreKit, developers do not have access to your payment details. You can request a refund directly from Apple by visiting reportaproblem.apple.com with your Apple Account.
