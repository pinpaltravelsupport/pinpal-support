❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 1 Free Photo Taste work?
Every traveler gets 1 free photo to test out the Photo Map on any state of their choice. To unlock photo fills across all 50 states and unlimited Polaroid vaults, upgrade to the Explorer Pass.
How do I restore my Explorer Pass on a new device?
Open Pinpal, tap the 👑 Explorer Pass button, and tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom of the modal. Your Apple ID will automatically restore your Lifetime or active subscription.
Are my trip photos and location data kept private?
Yes. Pinpal is built on a 100% private, local-first architecture. Your photos and travel lore are compressed and stored locally on your device storage (IndexedDB). We never sell your data to advertisers or third parties.
How do I manage or cancel an active subscription?
Subscriptions are managed directly by Apple. Go to your iPhone Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Pinpal to change plans or cancel at any time.
How does Cloud Sync work?
Tap the Cloud Sync button in the header to generate a private Sync Key. You can enter this key on your iPad, Mac, or secondary phone to keep your travel map synchronized.
