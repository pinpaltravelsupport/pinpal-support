❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to my logged trips and photos if I cancel or pause my subscription?
Your memories are yours forever. Zero photos, trips, or flight logs are ever deleted. All your uploaded Snapshot memories, state map covers, aviation records, and itinerary entries remain 100% saved and accessible on your device and in your private backups. If your subscription lapses, your account simply transitions to Read-Only mode: you retain full access to everything you've already created, but cannot add new photos or log new flights beyond the free tier limits until you renew.
What does the "Founder Lifetime" pass mean? Is it really lifetime?
The Founder Lifetime Pass is a one-time purchase with $0 recurring fees forever. It grants you non-expiring, VIP access to all Explorer Pass features, including current capabilities and upcoming updates released for the Pinpal application. You will never be billed again, and your access will never expire. We actively maintain and optimize Pinpal across new iPhone generations and major iOS releases. As standard in consumer software, "lifetime" refers to the commercial and operational lifecycle of the Pinpal application itself on supported platforms.
What happens if I get a new iPhone or switch devices?
Your purchases and data are fully portable:
  • Restoring Purchases: Tap the 👑 Explorer Pass button and select "Restore Purchases". Your Apple ID will immediately re-activate your Lifetime or active subscription.
  • Transferring Travel Data: You can transfer your travel map to a new phone either by using Cloud Sync (entering your private Sync Key) or by using the Export Data Backup tool in settings to save and import your JSON backup file.
How does the 1 Free Photo Taste work?
Every traveler receives 1 free photo slot to experience the Photo Map on any state of their choice. To unlock photo fills across all 50 states, unlimited Snapshot scrapbooks, and high-resolution Story Cards, you can upgrade to the Explorer Pass.
How do I restore my Explorer Pass on a new device?
Open Pinpal, tap the 👑 Explorer Pass button in the navigation bar, and tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom of the paywall modal. StoreKit will authenticate with your Apple ID and restore your entitlement automatically.
Are my trip photos and location data kept private?
Yes, 100%. Pinpal is built on a local-first privacy architecture. Your photos, flight logs, and travel notes are stored locally in your device's client-side storage (IndexedDB). Pinpal does not use background GPS tracking and never sells, rents, or shares your personal data with advertisers or third parties.
How do I manage or cancel an active subscription?
All subscriptions are billed and managed directly through Apple. To change or cancel your subscription at any time:
  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap your Name / Apple ID banner at the very top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select Pinpal, then tap Cancel Subscription.
How do I request a refund?
Because all financial transactions are processed by Apple's App Store via StoreKit, app developers do not have direct access to your payment details. You can request a refund directly from Apple within their standard refund window by visiting reportaproblem.apple.com using your Apple ID.
How does Cloud Sync work?
Tap the Cloud Sync icon in the top header to generate your private, anonymous Sync Key. Enter this key on your iPad, Mac, or secondary iPhone to keep your visited states and stats seamlessly synchronized without needing to create a password or social media account.
